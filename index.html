Image Editing

Image editing is where a lot of my digital design journey really started. I spent a lot of time figuring out how layers work, fixing colors, and putting different elements together to make something cool. For this section, I wanted to show a mix of things I've built, from custom backgrounds to hand-drawn pixel art and snowflakes. It took a lot of trial and error to get the details looking clean and sharp. Working on these pieces taught me how important patience is when you are trying to get a design just right.

Blender

Learning how to use Blender was definitely a big step up because going into a 3D space is totally different from flat drawing. You have to worry about shapes, lighting, and textures all at the same time. The projects here include a red car, a drum set, and a cupcake that I built from scratch. It was pretty challenging to shape everything correctly and make the materials look somewhat real. Looking back at these renders, I'm pretty proud of how far my 3D skills have come since I first opened the software.

3D modelling

This section focuses more on building structural objects and full environments rather than just single items. I worked on a bedroom scene to see how to place multiple furniture pieces together in a room without making it look messy. I also made simpler models like a box and a metal pipe to practice clean geometry and basic shapes. Building these helped me understand scale and proportion much better than before. It is cool to see how basic polygon shapes can be turned into recognizable everyday objects.

Game Development

Making games was probably one of the most fun parts of putting this whole tech portfolio together. It is completely different from designing art because you actually have to make things work and figure out why code is breaking. I spent a lot of hours testing out the controls, tweaking the gameplay, and fixing bugs so it runs smoothly. You can use the WASD keys to play the game right here on the page to see what it's like. Putting this together gave me a lot of respect for how much work goes into making even a simple game.
